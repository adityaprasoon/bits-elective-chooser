      Guide on Making the Elective Selection

      Several different approaches can help you make an informed elective selection. Consider the following guidance alongside your academic and career objectives.

      Possible approaches

      1. Choose according to the data your work produces.

        Prof. Saurabh's guiding rule is: “Pick electives by the data your world produces, not by the trendiest course name.”

        • Images and video → Computer Vision or Video Analysis
        • Text, documents, and logs → NLP, LLMs, or Agentic AI
        • Sensors and time series → Advanced Data Mining or ML on the Edge
        • Networks and relationships → Graph Neural Networks
      2. Prioritise subjects that require deep academic study.

        Subjects that are mathematical in nature or require rigorous study are often particularly suitable for an elective in this programme. Subjects that are mainly engineering-oriented, practical, or hands-on may also be learned through other resources, such as a Udemy course, especially because practical topics can change significantly within six months to a year.

      3. Review job requirements.

        Look at job listings in the field you plan to enter or advance in. Identify recurring requirements and compare them with the content of the available electives.

      Previous-batch resources

      Review the previous-batch elective handouts and browse the class recordings from previous batches. Going through the handouts and binge-watching selected recordings can help you understand the content of different electives and decide whether they are relevant to your career objectives.

      Choice planning aid only. Confirm course availability and academic requirements with your program.
